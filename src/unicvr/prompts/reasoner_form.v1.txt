## Field Rules

- **alternatives**: 0-2 genuinely different competing answers. Same type as best_answer. Each alternative must carry its own rationale explaining how it differs and why it is less likely.
- **state_factors**: 0-6. The witness question MUST directly adjudicate which of the two videos shows the earlier state — never a detail unrelated to their order (for pair [v4,v5], ask "Does v5 show the bowl filled before v4 adds to it?", not "Is the sauce smooth?").
